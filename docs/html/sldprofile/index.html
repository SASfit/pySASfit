SLD profile

pysasfit.sldprofile estimates the radial scattering-length-density profile ρ(r) of spheres, cylinders and lamellae from small-angle scattering data. Two routes are available:

  • direct Bayesian estimation of ρ(r) in one step (Hansen, J. Appl. Cryst. 49 (2016) 856), with a smoothness or maximum-entropy constraint and the noise level α and r_max from their posterior;

  • two-step (Glatter): indirect Fourier transformation I(q) → p(r), then deconvolution p(r) → ρ(r).

The profile is represented by shells (step profile) or by a quadratic or cubic B-spline, on uniform, power-law or adaptive grids. Projects and the last session are saved in versioned files; every panel can be detached into its own window.

The distance distribution p(r) alone (sphere, cylinder cross-section or lamella thickness, same normalisation) can also be obtained with every solver of the inversion tool, kernels sinc_4pi, cylinder_pddf and lamella_pddf (Forward model, data and information content).

The GUI is pysasfit-sldprofile, or the pySLDprofile tab of pysasfit-gui.
