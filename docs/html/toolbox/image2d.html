2D data

The tab 2D data of pyTOOLbox shows reduced 2D scattering images next to the radially averaged curves (module pysasfit.toolbox.image2d), with masking, averages, sums and rocking curves (module pysasfit.toolbox.analysis2d). The resulting curves go to the tab 1D data (1D data).

Show 2D in Merge geometries finds the image of a curve in memory (pySASred) or as <name>_2d.h5 next to its file.

The pySASred 2D NXcanSAS file contains I (ny, nx), Idev (statistical), Qx, Qy (I_axes = “Qy,Qx”), Mask (1 = not valid) and the pySASred reason bits (mask_bits), the detector geometry and, as in 1D, the parameters, notes and the header items of the raw run (SASprocess/metadata).

Display

  • qx – qy (equal axes, 1/nm), pixels (beam centre marked) or polar (ψ, q) (nearest-pixel resampling as in pySASred’s polar view);

  • colour scale linear, log(1+I), symlog or asinh, colour map, masked pixels grey, hidden or shown; the colour range follows the valid pixels;

  • q rings (list of q in 1/nm) drawn as circles (qx – qy) or lines (polar);

  • the cursor read-out gives the pixel, q, ψ and I ± dI, and whether the pixel is masked.

All views show the detector as seen looking towards the sample: +q_x to the right, +q_y up (Axis and angle conventions). ψ = 0 is the horizontal direction +q_x, increasing counter-clockwise, as in pySASred. The raw columns of SANS-I / SANS-LLB images run towards −x, so the pixel view shows column 0 on the right. pySASred 2D files written before 2026-11-23 (q_x of the other sign) are recognised and read in the present frame.

Beam centre

  • click in the image (pixels or q view, draw mode navigate) sets the beam centre there; or enter x, y [pixel] under Geometry of the image shown and press Apply geometry;

  • Centre of gravity refines the centre iteratively from the intensity around the current one.

q and ψ of every pixel follow the new centre, so the averages below use it at once.

Mask

Choose mask or unmask and the shape to draw — rectangle, ellipse or polygon — and draw it in any view (pixels, qx – qy or polar: a rectangle in the polar view is a sector of a ring); the pixels inside are masked or unmasked at once. navigate switches drawing off (zoom, pan, click for the beam centre). mask below / mask above masks pixels by value. Invert, Clear, Reset (back to the mask of the file), Undo, Suggest (pySASred’s automatic suggestion: beam stop, edges, dead and hot pixels) and Save / Load (HDF5, compatible with pySASred masks) act on the image shown.

two curves A(q), B(q) (magnetic SANS: nuclear and magnetic part)

Every bin is the mean of its valid pixels, σ = √(Σσᵢ²)/n. A + B sin² is a weighted linear least-squares fit of the ψ dependence in every q bin, with the uncertainties of A and B from the covariance. show region draws the sector, ring or box in the image. Compute → 1D data puts the curves in the tab 1D data.

Sum / rocking

  • Sum or Mean of the selected images (same detector; a pixel masked in any image stays masked) gives a new image in the list;

  • Rocking curve: the mean intensity in a region (whole detector, ring q1 – q2, or ring in the sector) of every selected image versus a header item (e.g. hdf:sample/omega) → 1D data.
