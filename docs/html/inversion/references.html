References

Literature relevant for the methods implemented in pysasfit.inversion, grouped by topic. Each entry notes where it is used in the package or in this documentation. DOIs are given where they were checked.

Small-angle scattering: indirect Fourier transformation and size distributions

  • O. Glatter (1977). A new method for the evaluation of small-angle scattering data. J. Appl. Cryst. 10, 415–421. – Indirect Fourier transformation with a cubic B-spline basis; basis of the B-spline option (Discretization of the integral).

  • O. Glatter (1980). Determination of particle-size distribution functions from small-angle scattering data by means of the indirect transformation method. J. Appl. Cryst. 13, 7–11. https://doi.org/10.1107/S0021889880011429 – Size distributions with the IFT approach (sphere kernel).

  • O. Glatter (1979). The interpretation of real-space information from small-angle scattering experiments. J. Appl. Cryst. 12, 166–175. – Pair-distance distributions of the cross-section and the thickness (rods and lamellae) and the corresponding transforms.

  • O. Glatter (1981). Convolution square root of band-limited symmetrical functions and its application to small-angle scattering data. J. Appl. Cryst. 14, 101–108. doi:10.1107/S002188988100887X – Deconvolution of \(p(r)\) into the radial density profile (DECON); two-step route of pysasfit.sldprofile.

  • O. Glatter & B. Hainisch (1984). Improvements in real-space deconvolution of small-angle scattering data. J. Appl. Cryst. 17, 435–441. doi:10.1107/S0021889884011894 – DECON with steps of variable width, influence of background.

  • D. I. Svergun, L. A. Feigin & B. M. Schedrin (1982). Small-angle scattering: direct structure analysis. Acta Cryst. A38, 827–835. – Direct determination of radial density profiles of centro-symmetric particles.

  • S. Hansen (2016). Estimation of the density distribution from small-angle scattering data. J. Appl. Cryst. 49, 856–865. https://doi.org/10.1107/S1600576716004969 – Kernels of the sphere, cylinder and lamella PDDFs (eqs. 1–5; the sinc_4pi, cylinder_pddf and lamella_pddf kernels), free end points for the lamella (hansen_eq19_even), and the direct Bayesian estimate of the density profile implemented in pysasfit.sldprofile.

  • R. Mittelbach & O. Glatter (1998). Direct structure analysis of small-angle scattering data from polydisperse colloidal particles. J. Appl. Cryst. 31, 600–608. – Density profiles from polydisperse systems.

  • C. L. P. Oliveira, B. B. Gerbelli, E. R. T. Silva, F. Nallet, L. Navailles, E. A. Oliveira & J. S. Pedersen (2012). Gaussian deconvolution: a useful method for a form-free modeling of scattering data from mono- and multilayered planar systems. J. Appl. Cryst. 45, 1278–1286. – Lamellar density profiles; comparison for the lamella kernel.

  • P. B. Moore (1980). Small-angle scattering. Information content and error analysis. J. Appl. Cryst. 13, 168–175. – Shannon sampling of \(I(q)\), number of independent parameters, sine-series expansion of \(p(r)\) (Forward model, data and information content, Discretization of the integral).

  • D. I. Svergun, A. V. Semenyuk & L. A. Feigin (1988). Small-angle- scattering-data treatment by the regularization method. Acta Cryst. A44, 244–250. – Tikhonov regularization for SAS data.

  • D. I. Svergun (1992). Determination of the regularization parameter in indirect-transform methods using perceptual criteria. J. Appl. Cryst. 25, 495–503. – GNOM; choice of the regularization parameter.

  • S. Hansen & J. S. Pedersen (1991). A comparison of three different methods for analysing small-angle scattering data. J. Appl. Cryst. 24, 541–548. – Comparison of IFT, maximum entropy and regularization for SAS.

  • S. Steenstrup & S. Hansen (1994). The maximum-entropy method without the positivity constraint – applications to the determination of the distance-distribution function in small-angle scattering. J. Appl. Cryst. 27, 574–580. – MaxEnt for signed \(p(r)\); background for the positive/negative entropy of the signed EM (EM, maximum entropy and signed kernels).

  • S. Hansen (2000). Bayesian estimation of hyperparameters for indirect Fourier transformation in small-angle scattering. J. Appl. Cryst. 33, 1415–1421. – Smoothness penalty with \(p(0)=p(D_{max})=0\) (eq. 19 and the matrix stated in the text), Bayesian choice of \(\lambda\) (bayesian_evidence, Regularization and choice of the regularization strength).

  • B. Vestergaard & S. Hansen (2006). Application of Bayesian analysis to indirect Fourier transformation in small-angle scattering. J. Appl. Cryst. 39, 797–804. – Evidence formula and number of good parameters \(N_g\) (pysasfit.inversion.solvers.bayesian_evidence).

  • S. Hansen (2012). BayesApp: a web site for indirect transformation of small-angle scattering data. J. Appl. Cryst. 45, 566–567. – Reference implementation of Bayesian IFT.

  • A. H. Larsen & M. C. Pedersen (2021). Experimental noise in small-angle scattering can be assessed using the Bayesian indirect Fourier transformation. J. Appl. Cryst. 54, 1281–1289. https://doi.org/10.1107/S1600576721006877 – Estimating and rescaling the error level with Bayesian IFT; related to the evidence-based error scale (noise_scale="estimate").

  • J. A. Potton, G. J. Daniell & B. D. Rainford (1988). A new method for the determination of particle size distributions from small angle neutron scattering measurements. J. Appl. Cryst. 21, 891ff. https://doi.org/10.1107/S0021889888004595 – Maximum entropy for SANS size distributions.

  • P. R. Jemian, J. R. Weertman, G. G. Long & R. D. Spal (1991). Characterization of 9Cr-1MoVNb steel by anomalous small-angle X-ray scattering. Acta Metall. Mater. 39, 2477–2487. – Maximum-entropy size distributions (the MaxEnt method of Irena).

  • J. S. Pedersen (1994). Determination of size distributions from small-angle scattering data for systems with effective hard-sphere interactions. J. Appl. Cryst. 27, 595–608. – Size distributions by regularized least squares.

  • B. R. Pauw, J. S. Pedersen, S. Tardif, M. Takata & B. B. Iversen (2013). Improvements and considerations for size distribution retrieval from small-angle scattering data by Monte Carlo methods. J. Appl. Cryst. 46, 365–371. – Monte Carlo alternative to regularized inversion (McSAS).

  • I. Bressler, B. R. Pauw & A. F. Thünemann (2015). McSAS: software for the retrieval of model parameter distributions from scattering patterns. J. Appl. Cryst. 48, 962–969. https://doi.org/10.1107/S1600576715007347

  • I. Breßler, J. Kohlbrecher & A. F. Thünemann (2015). SASfit: a tool for small-angle scattering data analysis using a library of analytical expressions. J. Appl. Cryst. 48, 1587–1598.

  • J. Kohlbrecher & I. Breßler (2022). Updates in SASfit for fitting analytical expressions and numerical models to small-angle scattering patterns. J. Appl. Cryst. 55, 1677–1688. https://doi.org/10.1107/S1600576722009037 – The “JAC 2022 paper” of the code comments: EM, EM + smoothing, EM + MaxEnt with constant and adaptive prior (eq. 36–55), size weighting \(x=N\,R^\alpha\), G-test, the SASfit C solvers this package ports (em_discrepancy, maxent_em).

  • D. Franke, C. M. Jeffries & D. I. Svergun (2015). Correlation Map, a goodness-of-fit test for one-dimensional X-ray scattering spectra. Nature Methods 12, 419–422. https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.3358 – Goodness of fit without error bars (run-length test); an alternative to the statistics in pysasfit.inversion.goodness_of_fit.

Books on small-angle scattering

  • O. Glatter & O. Kratky (eds.) (1982). Small Angle X-ray Scattering. Academic Press, London.

  • L. A. Feigin & D. I. Svergun (1987). Structure Analysis by Small-Angle X-ray and Neutron Scattering. Plenum Press, New York.

  • A. Guinier & G. Fournet (1955). Small-Angle Scattering of X-rays. Wiley, New York.

  • P. Debye (1915). Zerstreuung von Röntgenstrahlen. Ann. Phys. 351 (4. Folge 46), 809–823. – Orientational average, \(j_0(qr)\) kernel.

Ill-posed problems and regularization

  • D. L. Phillips (1962). A technique for the numerical solution of certain integral equations of the first kind. J. ACM 9, 84–97.

  • S. Twomey (1963). On the numerical solution of Fredholm integral equations of the first kind by the inversion of the linear system produced by quadrature. J. ACM 10, 97–101.

  • A. N. Tikhonov (1963). Solution of incorrectly formulated problems and the regularization method. Soviet Math. Dokl. 4, 1035–1038.

  • A. N. Tikhonov & V. Y. Arsenin (1977). Solutions of Ill-Posed Problems. Winston, Washington.

  • V. A. Morozov (1966). On the solution of functional equations by the method of regularization. Soviet Math. Dokl. 7, 414–417. – Discrepancy principle (Regularization and choice of the regularization strength).

  • G. H. Golub, M. Heath & G. Wahba (1979). Generalized cross-validation as a method for choosing a good ridge parameter. Technometrics 21, 215–223. – GCV (tikhonov_standard_gcv, tsvd_gcv, general_tikhonov_gcv).

  • P. C. Hansen (1992). Analysis of discrete ill-posed problems by means of the L-curve. SIAM Review 34, 561–580.

  • P. C. Hansen & D. P. O’Leary (1993). The use of the L-curve in the regularization of discrete ill-posed problems. SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 14, 1487–1503.

  • A. Cultrera & L. Callegaro (2020). A simple algorithm to find the L-curve corner in the regularisation of ill-posed inverse problems. IOP SciNotes 1, 025004. https://doi.org/10.1088/2633-1357/abad0d – Menger-curvature corner criterion (pysasfit.inversion.lambda_selection).

  • P. C. Hansen (1994). Regularization Tools: a Matlab package for analysis and solution of discrete ill-posed problems. Numer. Algorithms 6, 1–35; version 4.0: Numer. Algorithms 46 (2007), 189–194. – std_form, filter factors, maxent.m (transcribed in general_tikhonov, svd_methods, hansen_maxent).

  • P. C. Hansen (1998). Rank-Deficient and Discrete Ill-Posed Problems: Numerical Aspects of Linear Inversion. SIAM, Philadelphia.

  • P. C. Hansen (2010). Discrete Inverse Problems: Insight and Algorithms. SIAM, Philadelphia.

  • H. W. Engl, M. Hanke & A. Neubauer (1996). Regularization of Inverse Problems. Kluwer, Dordrecht.

  • M. Donatelli & L. Reichel (2014). Square smoothing regularization matrices with accurate boundary conditions. J. Comput. Appl. Math. 272, 334–349. – Boundary conditions of the difference operators (pysasfit.inversion.regularization).

  • R. E. Jones (2023). ARLS – Automatically Regularized Least Squares. MATLAB Central File Exchange no. 130259; described in C. Moler’s blog Cleve’s Corner, 16 June 2023. – arlsnn (discrete Picard condition, no parameter to choose).

Bayesian inference and maximum entropy

  • D. J. C. MacKay (1992). Bayesian interpolation. Neural Computation 4, 415–447. – Evidence framework; joint estimation of the regularization and noise hyperparameters (error scale \(s\)).

  • J. Skilling & R. K. Bryan (1984). Maximum entropy image reconstruction: general algorithm. Mon. Not. R. Astron. Soc. 211, 111–124.

  • S. F. Gull (1989). Developments in maximum entropy data analysis. In J. Skilling (ed.), Maximum Entropy and Bayesian Methods, pp. 53–71. Kluwer, Dordrecht.

  • S. F. Gull & J. Skilling (1990). The MEMSYS5 Users’ Manual. Maximum Entropy Data Consultants Ltd, Royston. – Positive/negative entropy.

  • M. P. Hobson & A. N. Lasenby (1998). The entropic prior for distributions with positive and negative values. Mon. Not. R. Astron. Soc. 298, 905–908. – Derivation of \(S_\pm=\sum[\psi-2m-p\,\mathrm{asinh}(p/2m)]\) used by the signed EM + MaxEnt.

  • K. Horne (1985). Images of accretion discs. I. The eclipse mapping method. Mon. Not. R. Astron. Soc. 213, 129–141. – Default model from a smoothed version of the image (adaptive prior of maxent_em).

EM algorithm and related iterative methods

  • W. H. Richardson (1972). Bayesian-based iterative method of image restoration. J. Opt. Soc. Am. 62, 55–59.

  • L. B. Lucy (1974). An iterative technique for the rectification of observed distributions. Astron. J. 79, 745–754.

  • A. P. Dempster, N. M. Laird & D. B. Rubin (1977). Maximum likelihood from incomplete data via the EM algorithm. J. R. Stat. Soc. B 39, 1–38.

  • L. A. Shepp & Y. Vardi (1982). Maximum likelihood reconstruction for emission tomography. IEEE Trans. Med. Imaging 1, 113–122.

  • Y. Vardi & D. Lee (1993). From image deblurring to optimal investments: maximum likelihood solutions for positive linear inverse problems. J. R. Stat. Soc. B 55, 569–598. – EM for Fredholm equations with non-negative kernels.

  • B. W. Silverman, M. C. Jones, J. D. Wilson & D. W. Nychka (1990). A smoothed EM approach to indirect estimation problems, with particular reference to stereology and emission tomography. J. R. Stat. Soc. B 52, 271–324. – EMS: EM with a smoothing step after every iteration (em_discrepancy).

  • D. W. Nychka (1990). Some properties of adding a smoothing step to the EM algorithm. Stat. Probab. Lett. 9, 187–193.

  • P. J. Green (1990). Bayesian reconstructions from emission tomography data using a modified EM algorithm. IEEE Trans. Med. Imaging 9, 84–93. – One-step-late EM for penalized likelihood.

  • P. P. B. Eggermont & V. N. LaRiccia (1995). Maximum smoothed likelihood density estimation for inverse problems. Ann. Statist. 23, 199–220.

  • P. P. B. Eggermont (1999). Nonlinear smoothing and the EM algorithm for positive integral equations of the first kind. Appl. Math. Optim. 39, 75–91.

  • C. Byrne & P. P. B. Eggermont (2011). EM algorithms. In O. Scherzer (ed.), Handbook of Mathematical Methods in Imaging, pp. 271–344. Springer, New York.

  • M. Chae, R. Martin & S. G. Walker (2019). On an algorithm for solving Fredholm integrals of the first kind. Stat. Comput. 29, 645–654. – Shift and kernel split for signed solutions and kernels (em_general_signed_kernel, EM, maximum entropy and signed kernels).

  • D. S. C. Biggs & M. Andrews (1995). Conjugate gradient acceleration of maximum-likelihood image restoration. Electron. Lett. 31, 1985–1986.

  • D. S. C. Biggs & M. Andrews (1997). Acceleration of iterative image restoration algorithms. Appl. Opt. 36, 1766–1775. – Vector extrapolation used for all EM-type iterations (pysasfit.inversion.solvers.acceleration).

  • D. S. C. Biggs (1998). Accelerated Iterative Blind Deconvolution. PhD thesis, University of Auckland. http://hdl.handle.net/2292/1760

Splines, interpolation and quadrature

  • C. de Boor (1972). On calculating with B-splines. J. Approx. Theory 6, 50–62.

  • M. G. Cox (1972). The numerical evaluation of B-splines. IMA J. Appl. Math. 10, 134–149.

  • C. de Boor (1978). A Practical Guide to Splines. Springer, New York. – Cox–de Boor recursion, not-a-knot end conditions (cubic_basis(), pysasfit.inversion.spline_basis).

  • F. N. Fritsch & R. E. Carlson (1980). Monotone piecewise cubic interpolation. SIAM J. Numer. Anal. 17, 238–246. – PCHIP; discussed and not used (non-linear in the node values).

  • C. E. Shannon (1949). Communication in the presence of noise. Proc. IRE 37, 10–21. – Sampling theorem, Shannon number.

Error estimation and goodness of fit

  • A. Savitzky & M. J. E. Golay (1964). Smoothing and differentiation of data by simplified least squares procedures. Anal. Chem. 36, 1627–1639. – Local polynomial least-squares fits; basis of the 5-point cubic error estimate (estimate_errors(model="poly")).

  • J. Rice (1984). Bandwidth choice for nonparametric regression. Ann. Statist. 12, 1215–1230. – Difference-based estimate of the noise variance.

  • T. Gasser, L. Sroka & C. Jennen-Steinmetz (1986). Residual variance and residual pattern in nonlinear regression. Biometrika 73, 625–633. – Pseudo-residuals from the interpolation between neighbours, the 3-point estimate (estimate_errors(model="scatter")).

  • P. Hall, J. W. Kay & D. M. Titterington (1990). Asymptotically optimal difference-based estimation of variance in nonparametric regression. Biometrika 77, 521–528. https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/77.3.521

  • P. J. Rousseeuw & C. Croux (1993). Alternatives to the median absolute deviation. J. Am. Stat. Assoc. 88, 1273–1283. – Median absolute deviation, factor 1.4826 for Gaussian noise.

  • K. Pearson (1900). On the criterion that a given system of deviations from the probable in the case of a correlated system of variables is such that it can be reasonably supposed to have arisen from random sampling. Phil. Mag. (5) 50, 157–175. – Pearson \(\chi^2\).

  • S. S. Wilks (1938). The large-sample distribution of the likelihood ratio for testing composite hypotheses. Ann. Math. Stat. 9, 60–62. – Likelihood-ratio (G) test.

  • S. Kullback & R. A. Leibler (1951). On information and sufficiency. Ann. Math. Stat. 22, 79–86. – KL divergence.

  • B. Efron (1979). Bootstrap methods: another look at the jackknife. Ann. Statist. 7, 1–26. – Bootstrap (Uncertainty of the recovered distribution).

Numerical optimization

  • R. P. Brent (1973). Algorithms for Minimization without Derivatives. Prentice-Hall, Englewood Cliffs. – Root bracketing and Brent’s method in the discrepancy-principle searches.

  • R. H. Byrd, P. Lu, J. Nocedal & C. Zhu (1995). A limited memory algorithm for bound constrained optimization. SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 16, 1190–1208. – L-BFGS-B (hansen_maxent).

  • J. Nocedal & S. J. Wright (2006). Numerical Optimization, 2nd ed. Springer, New York. – Newton polish, Armijo backtracking.
