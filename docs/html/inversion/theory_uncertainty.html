Uncertainty of the recovered distribution

Analytic (Laplace) error band

For the Bayesian-evidence solver the posterior is Gaussian, with covariance equal to the inverse Hessian of \(\lambda S+\chi^2/2\) at the MAP point:

\[\mathrm{Cov}(\mathbf p) = \left(2\lambda\mathbf L^T\mathbf L + \mathbf A^T\mathbf W\mathbf A\right)^{-1},\qquad \sigma_{p_j} = \sqrt{\mathrm{Cov}_{jj}} .\]

It contains the noise propagation and the prior’s uncertainty for a fixed \(\lambda\), but not the uncertainty of \(\lambda\) itself. In B-spline mode the coefficient covariance is mapped to the display grid with \(\mathbf B\,\mathrm{Cov}(\mathbf c)\,\mathbf B^T\). The band is shown automatically (“MAP solution” with \(\pm1\sigma\)).

Parametric bootstrap

pysasfit.inversion.uncertainty.bootstrap_uncertainty() works for every solver (including the non-linear EM and MaxEnt solvers and the automatic choice of the regularization parameter, which is repeated for every replicate):

\[\mathbf b^{(k)} = \hat{\mathbf b} + \boldsymbol\varepsilon^{(k)},\qquad \varepsilon^{(k)}_i\sim\mathcal N(0,\sigma_i^2),\qquad k=1\dots N_{boot},\]
\[\bar{\mathbf x} = \frac1{N_{boot}}\sum_k\mathbf x^{(k)},\qquad \sigma_{x_j}^2 = \frac1{N_{boot}}\sum_k\left(x^{(k)}_j-\bar x_j\right)^2 .\]

Replicates whose solve fails are skipped and counted. The relative error of \(\sigma_{x_j}\) is about \(1/\sqrt{2N_{boot}}\) (10 % for \(N_{boot}=50\)).

Centre of the replicates

The replicates are generated around the fitted curve \(\hat{\mathbf b}=\mathbf A\mathbf x\) of the single solve, not around the measured \(\mathbf b\). The measured data already contain one realization of noise; \(\mathbf b+\boldsymbol\varepsilon\) has variance \(2\sigma_i^2\) and every replicate a true \(\chi^2_r\approx2\). A solver forced to \(\chi^2_r=1\) must then remove most of its regularization on such replicates: on test.dat centring on \(\mathbf b\) drove \(\lambda\) down by up to \(10^8\) (std of \(p\) up to 5.9 for a peak of 1), centring on the fit gave std \(\le0.014\). The floor \(\lambda\ge10^{-3}\lambda_{ev}\) of the forced-\(\chi^2\) search is a second safeguard.

The GUI plots the bootstrap mean with a \(\pm1\sigma\) band and the single solve as a dashed line; for non-linear solvers the two need not coincide.
